Privacy policy

Your privacy, plain and simple

RoadTripRoutes · Last reviewed September 18, 2026

TL;DR

RoadTripRoutes is an informational site. We do not set tracking cookies today, we do not sell your data, and we have no login, no accounts, no newsletter signup. If you email us, we hold what you send for as long as needed to help you. That’s most of the policy; below is the precise version.

Who we are

RoadTripRoutes (“we”, “us”, “RoadTripRoutes”) is an independent, informational website about road trips and weekend getaways in the United States, published from the United States and available worldwide. This policy explains what data we touch and why. It applies to the site only, not to third-party sites we link to.

What we collect

Today: almost nothing

The site runs no accounts, no comment system, no newsletter and no contact forms that store submissions server-side. The data we touch is limited to:

  • Email correspondence — if you write to hello@roadtriproutes.com, we hold your message and address as long as needed to respond and to keep a reasonable record of a correction; typically up to two years.
  • Consent choice — your banner choice is saved in your own browser’s localStorage (see “Cookies & consent”). It never reaches our servers.
  • Basic server logs — like most sites, the hosting server records requests (IP address, browser type, pages requested) for security and to detect abuse. These are not used for profiling and are retained only as long as operationally necessary.

Potential future: ads and analytics

The site may later show advertising (for example, Google AdSense) and read basic aggregated analytics (for example, Google Analytics). If that happens it may set cookies or use storage, and your consent choice from our banner will apply — nothing is loaded until you choose, and “necessary only” keeps the site clean of ads and non-essential analytics.

Cookies & consent

You control this. The first time you visit, a small banner asks you to choose between “Accept all” and “Necessary only”. Your choice is stored in your browser’s localStorage and applied to any future advertising/analytics on the site. You can change your choice any time via the “Cookie & consent settings” link in the footer.

How we use data

  • To answer your email and act on corrections.
  • To keep the site secure and functioning.
  • If advertising is enabled, to serve ads within the limits you consented to.

We never sell personal information, and we don’t use your data for anything we haven’t described here.

Your rights (GDPR & CCPA-aware)

If you are in the EEA / UK / Switzerland (GDPR)

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of the personal data we hold about you; you may object to or restrict processing, and request portability where it applies. Our lawful bases are legitimate interest (operating the site, security) and consent (banner choices and any future ads/analytics). You can withdraw consent at any time.

If you are in California (CCPA / CPRA)

You have the right to know what personal information we collect, the right to request deletion, and the right to non-discrimination for exercising your rights. We do not “sell” or “share” personal information as those terms are defined by the CCPA — no data is exchanged for money or to target advertising. Do Not Track signals are honored to the extent that we set no tracking technology in the first place.

To exercise any right, email hello@roadtriproutes.com with “Privacy” in the subject. We will verify your identity with reasonable means before acting, and respond within the timeframes required by law (usually within 30–45 days).

Children

The site is general travel information and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Third-party links

Our guides link to official sources (NPS, AAA, DOT, state toll authorities) and to photo credits. Those sites have their own privacy policies and are not covered by this one. Please check the destination before you share anything on them.

Security, retention & changes

We apply reasonable technical and organizational measures for the little data we hold. Data is kept only as long as needed for the purpose described, then deleted. We may update this policy; material changes will be announced with a new “last reviewed” date on this page, so check back occasionally.

Questions? Contact us — hello@roadtriproutes.com.

This policy is a plain-language explanation of our practices. It does not create contractual rights for any party. Nothing here guarantees a specific outcome of any legal request, and local laws may differ.
