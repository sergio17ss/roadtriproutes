Contact

Talk to the desk

By Editorial RoadTripRoutes · Last reviewed September 18, 2026

The fastest way to reach us is email: hello@roadtriproutes.com. We’re a small desk, so while we read everything, reply times are best measured in working days rather than minutes.

  • Corrections & stale numbers Spotted a fee that changed, a closure that’s wrong, a toll that’s been lifted? Tell us the page and the figure — we verify against the official source and publish a dated correction. See the corrections policy.
  • Route questions & requests A drive you wish someone would price properly? Suggest it — we keep a public wish-list of routes under research.
  • Privacy & data requests For access, deletion or any question about your data under GDPR/CCPA, email hello@ with “Privacy” in the subject. Full details are in the privacy policy.
  • Media & partnerships Interviews, republishing requests and advertising inquiries — the same address works. We keep advertising strictly separated from editorial; see editorial guidelines.

Not a booking or emergency service

RoadTripRoutes is an informational site. We cannot make reservations, refund tickets or change park passes — and for anything urgent on the road, contact the relevant agency or emergency services directly (in the US, call 911 for emergencies).

Emails are read by the RoadTripRoutes editorial desk. Please don’t include sensitive personal data in unsolicited correspondence. For CCPA/GDPR requests we will need to verify your identity before acting; details are in the privacy policy.
